Solving by Substitution

\(\begin{cases} x-y=1 \\ 2x+3y=17 \end{cases}\)

Solve one equation for one variable.

Pick the easier one!!

\(x-y=1 \\ x = y + 1\)

Substitute the result into the other eq.

After substituting, you should only have
one variable in your equation.

\(2(y+1)+3y=17 \\ 2y+2 + 3y = 17 \\ 5y = 15 \\ y = 3\)

Back-substitute the (2) answer into the (1) answer to find the other variable.

\(x=y+1=3+1=4\)

Check your solution.

\(x=4, y=3\)

Solving by Elimination

\(\begin{cases} x-y=1 \\ 2x+3y=17 \end{cases}\)

Pick a variable to eliminate.

Pick the easier one!!

\(3\cdot (x-y=1) \\ 3x -3y = 3\)

Add the equations to cancel out one variable, and solve for the remaining variable.

\( \begin{array}{cccc} &3x -3y = 3 \\ + &2x + 3y = 17 \\ \hline &5x = 20 \\ &x = 4 \end{array}\)

Back-substitute your (2) solution into one of the original eqs.

\(x-y=1 \\ 4-y=1 \\ y=3\)

Check your solution.
