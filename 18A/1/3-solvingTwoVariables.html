Solving by Substitution

  1. Isolate one variable: solve one equation for one variable.

    Pick the easier one!!

  2. Substitute: replace the variable you isolated in the other equation.

    After substituting, you should only have
    one variable in your equation.

  3. Back-substitute: plug the (2) answer into the (1) answer to find the other variable.

  4. Check your solution in BOTH eqs!

Solving by Elimination

  1. Pick a variable to eliminate.

    Pick the easier one!!

  2. Add the equations to cancel out one variable, and solve for the remaining variable.

  3. Back-substitute your (2) solution into one of the original eqs.

  4. Check your solution in BOTH eqs!
