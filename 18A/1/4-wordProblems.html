Example 4

You have 5 apples and 4 oranges. (Caution: order matters!)

a

You have 1 more apple(s) than oranges.

b

You have 1 fewer orange(s) than apples.

c

The above difference is 20% of the 5 apples.

difference\(/\)total apples \(=1/5=0.2=20\%\)

c

The above difference is 25% of the 4 oranges.

difference\(/\)total oranges \(=1/4=0.25=25\%\)

Percentage Rules

Order matters.

  • A is P% more than B

    applies the percent to B

    \(\leftarrow\) NOT the same as B is P% less than A

    \(A = \left( 1 + \frac{P}{100} \right) \cdot B \)

  • A is P% less than B

    applies the percent to A

    \(\leftarrow\) NOT the same as B is P% more than A

    \(A = \left( 1 - \frac{P}{100} \right) \cdot B \)
