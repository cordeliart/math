Example 3a: Traditional Method

Write an equation with the two points.

  1. find slope \(m\)

    \(m= \dfrac{y_2-y_1}{x_2-x_1}= \dfrac{5-1}{3-1} = \dfrac{4}{2} = 2\)

  2. plug one point to solve for \(b\)

    \(y = mx + b\) using \((1,1)\)

    \((1)=2(1) + b\)

    \(1-2=b\)

    \(-1=b\)

    \(y=2x-1\)
