What’s the difference between an expression and an equation?

An expression is made of terms combined with \(+\) and \(-\).

An equation is made of expressions combined with an equal sign.

What is a term in an expression/equation?

Some combination of a number/fraction with a variable (possibly with an exponent), like \(3x^2\) or \(y^7\) or \(6\).

Polynomial

A polynomial, meaning “multiple terms,” is a type of expression that only has terms with a variable (like \(x\)) with an exponent and a coefficient

Ex: \(7x^3+x+1\)
Degree (Review from 1.2)

the degree of a polynomial the highest exponent on the variable

Ex: \(3x^4 + 2x +7\) is degree 4
Leading Term

the leading term is the term with the highest exponent

Ex: \(3x^4 + 2x +7\) has leading term \(3x^4\)
Quadratic

a quadratic is a polynomial of degree 2

Ex: \(5x^2 - 8x +1\)

Adding & Subtracting Polynomials

When simplifying polynomials, we combine like terms by adding/subtracting terms with the same variable+exponent.

$$f(x) = -8x^7 + 3x^7 - 4x^2 + 2x$$
$$f(x) = (-8 + 3)x^7 - 4x^2 + 2x$$
$$f(x) = -5x^7 - 4x^2 + 2x$$

Adding & Subtracting Polynomials

When simplifying polynomials, we combine like terms by adding/subtracting terms with the same variable+exponent.

$$g(x) = 8x^7 - 3x^7 - 4x^2 + 2x$$
$$g(x) = (8 - 3)x^7 - 4x^2 + 2x$$
$$g(x) = 5x^7 - 4x^2 + 2x$$

Multiplying Polynomials

When multiplying polynomials, we use the Distributive Property repeatedly and then combine like terms.

$$a(b + c) = ab + ac$$
$$(b + c)a = ba + ca$$

Special Cases

There are two special cases for factoring polynomials. If you can learn to recognize these patterns, it will make your life much easier!

Perfect Squares
$$ (ax+b)(ax+b) $$
Difference of Squares
$$ (ax+b)(ax-b) $$
