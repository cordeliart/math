Method 2:
Quadratic Formula

The Quadratic Formula

Not every polynomial can be factored. In which case, the solutions are given by the formula:

$$ x = \dfrac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2-4ac} }{2a} $$

Downsides

When we use the quadratic formula, we might get an imaginary number as a solution, instead of a real number.

All imaginary numbers are of the form \(ai + b\), where \(i=\sqrt{-1}\).
