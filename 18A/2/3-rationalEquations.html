What does rational mean?

What does rational mean?

Rational refers to fractions.

Rational Equation

a rational equation is one that has a variable in the denominator

Ex: \(y= \dfrac{1}{x}+3\)

Clearing Fractions

We carefully multiply the whole equation by each denominator to cancel all of them out.

  1. multiply by each denominator

  2. cancel out the fractions

  3. rewrite the fraction-less equation

  4. solve as usual

\( \color{#111111} \big(\) \( \dfrac{1}{x} -5 = \dfrac{2}{x} \) \( \color{#111111}\big) \cdot x \)

\( \dfrac{1 {\color{#111111}\cdot x}}{x} - 5 {\color{#111111}\cdot x} = \dfrac{2 {\color{#111111}\cdot x}}{x} \)

\( 1-5x = 2 \)

\( x=-2/5 \)
