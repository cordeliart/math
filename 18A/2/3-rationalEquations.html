What does rational mean?

What does rational mean?

Rational refers to fractions.

Rational Equation

a rational equation is one that has a variable in the denominator

Ex: \(y= \dfrac{1}{x}+3\)

Clearing Fractions

We carefully multiply the whole equation by each denominator to cancel all of them out.

  1. multiply by each denominator

  2. cancel out the fractions

  3. rewrite the fraction-less equation

  4. solve as usual

\( \color{#386ef6} \big(\) \( \dfrac{1}{x} -5 = \dfrac{2}{x} \) \( \color{#386ef6}\big) \cdot x \)

\( \dfrac{1 {\color{#386ef6}\cdot x}}{x} - 5 {\color{#386ef6}\cdot x} = \dfrac{2 {\color{#386ef6}\cdot x}}{x} \)

\( 1-5x = 2 \)

\( x=-2/5 \)

Extraneous Solutions

Extraneous Solution

an extraneous solution is a result that does not work when plugged into the original equation

Extraneous Solution

an extraneous solution is a result that does not work when plugged into the original equation

Don't box an extraneous solution as part of your final answer!!

When to Watch Out

  • zeroes in denominators

    \(1 = \frac{1}{x-2}\)   CANNOT have \(x=2\) as a solution!

  • roots of negative numbers

    \(x = \sqrt{-4}\)   has an imaginary solution

Do not be too quick to claim extraneous!! There is a chance your solution is not working because you made a mistake when 1) trying to solve, or 2) trying to check your answer.
