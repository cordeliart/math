Parabola

a parabola is the U-shaped graph that a quadratic makes

Vertex

the vertex is the maximum or minimum of the parabola

Axis of Symmetry

the axis of symmetry is the vertical line that splits a parabola into perfectly symmetrical halves

Reading Vertex Form

Vertex Form
$$ f(x) = a (x-h)^2 + k $$

The vertex of the parabola is at \( (h,k) \).

Note: the \(a\) is the same one in \(ax^2+bx+c\)!

Completing the Square

  1. Identify \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\). If \(a\neq 0\), factor out \(a\).

    \(f(x) = x^2 - 2x - 3\)

  2. Calculate \(h=-b/2a\).

    \( h = -(-2)/(2\cdot 1) = \)\(1\)

  3. Add AND subtract \(h^2\) (add “\(0\)”).

    \( f(x)= \) \(x^2 - 2x\)\(+(1)^2\)\(-(1)^2\)\(-3\)

  4. Swap \(x^2\pm\frac{b}{a}x+h^2\) into perfect squares form \((x\pm h)^2\).

    \( f(x) = \) \((x-1)^2 \)\(-(1)^2\)\(-3\)

  5. Distribute \(a\), and simplify.

    \( f(x) = (x-1)^2-2\)

Completing the Square (Template)

  1. Identify \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\). If \(a\neq 0\), factor out \(a\).

    \(f(x) = ax^2 + bx +c \)

    \( f(x) = a(x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x + \frac{c}{a})\)

  2. Calculate \(h=-b/2a\).

  3. Add AND subtract \(h^2\) (add “\(0\)”).

    \( f(x)= a(\)\(x^2 + \frac{b}{a}x\)\(+h^2\)\(-h^2\)\(-\frac{c}{a})\)

  4. Swap \(x^2\pm\frac{b}{a}x+h^2\) into perfect squares form \((x\pm h)^2\).

    \( f(x) = a(\)\((x+ h)^2 \)\(-h^2\)\(-\frac{c}{a})\)

  5. Distribute \(a\), and simplify.

    \( f(x) = a(x+h)^2-h^2a+c\)

Switching from ABC to Vertex

  1. Identify \(a\), \(b\), and \(c\)

  2. Calculate \(h=-b/2a\)

  3. Calculate \(k=f(h)\) (plug in \(x=h\))

  4. Plug \(a\), \(h\), and \(k\) into vertex form \(f(x) = a(x-h)^2+k\), and watch out for double negatives when plugging in \(h\)!

Note: the chart on p.52 is wrong; the formula for \(h\) should have a negative.

Sketching Graphs

  1. Plot and label the vertex in the correct quadrant

    It does not need to be to scale! Do not draw a bunch of tick marks. Labeling the point with an ordered pair is enough.

  2. Plot and label the y-intercept

    ABC: at \( (0,c) \)
    Vertex: plug in \(x=0\) and solve

  3. Connect points with a parabola

    Check that the way it opens matches the sign of \(a\)!
