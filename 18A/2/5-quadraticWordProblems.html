Example 1

A rocket is launched from atop a 101-foot cliff with an initial velocity of 116 ft/s.

a

What is the equation for this problem?

\(h(t) = -16t^2 + 116t + 101\)

b

Use the quadratic formula find out how long the rocket will take to hit the ground after it is launched.

\(t=8\) seconds

Example 2

An object is launched directly upward at 64 ft/s from a platform 80 feet high.

a

When will it attain its maximum height?

\(h(t) = -16t^2 + 64t + 80\)

\(t =2\) seconds

b

What will be the object’s maximum height?

\(h=144\) feet
