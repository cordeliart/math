Switching Up Variables

\(x\)

\(y\)

\(t\) (time)

\(h\) (height)

57
a

How would we find the max or min height of a quadratic?

The \(\boldsymbol{y}\)-value of the vertex (\(k\) in vertex form)

b

How would we find the time it is at a specific height?

The \(\boldsymbol{x}\)-value of the vertex (\(h\) in vertex form)

  • OR plug in \(y=\) max (or min) and solve for \(x\)

(Lots of) Examples

57-58
Example 1

A rocket is launched from atop a 101-foot cliff with an initial velocity of 116 ft/s.

a

What is the equation for this problem?

\(h(t) = -16t^2 + 116t + 101\)

b

Use the quadratic formula find out how long the rocket will take to hit the ground after it is launched.

asking for: the positive real zero   \(t=8\) seconds
