Division Algorithm Format

Division Algorithm Format

$$ \text{dividend} = \text{divisor} \cdot \text{quotient} + \text{remainder}$$
$$ \frac{123}{7} = 17 + \frac{4}{7} $$
➔
$$ 123 = 7\cdot 17 +4$$

Division Algorithm Format

$$ \text{dividend} = \text{divisor} \cdot \text{quotient} + \text{remainder}$$
$$ \frac{7}{2} = 3 + \frac{1}{2} $$
➔
$$ 7 = 2\cdot 3 +1$$

If all the numbers are positive and the remainder \(<\) divisor, then this equation is unique for every pair of dividend and divisor.

Division Algorithm: Polynomials

  1. Write in descending order. Fill in any missing terms with zero.

  2. What do you need to match first term ONLY? Put that on top.

  3. Distribute that term to entire divisor, & line up underneath the dividend.

  4. Subtract expression from dividend.

  5. Repeat until remaining degree \(<\) divisor.
