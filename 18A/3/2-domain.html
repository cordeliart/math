Example 4

Find the domain of \( f(x) = \dfrac{ x+1 }{ x^2-4x-5 }\) & express in interval notation.

Danger zone: when bottom \(=0\)

\( x^2 - 4x - 5 \neq 0\)

\( (x -5)(x+1) \neq 0\)

\( x\neq -1,5\)

\(D = ( -\infty, -1)\cup (-1,5) \cup (5,\infty) \)
