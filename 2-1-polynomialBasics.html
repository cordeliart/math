2.1 Polynomial Basics

What is a polynomial?

Polynomial

The word polynomial literally means "many terms."

Polynomial

The word polynomial literally means "many terms."

A term consists of a number, called a coefficient, multiplied by a variable with a positive, whole number power.

Terms are separated by \(+\) and \(-\); when separating or rearranging terms, you should keep minuses attached as a negative coefficient.
$$f(x) = 3x^3-5x+2$$

Polynomial Degree

The degree of a polynomial is the highest power on the variable.

$$f(x)=3x^3- 5x+2$$
$$f(x)=3x^{\color{#386ef6} 3} -5x+2$$

2nd degree

Quadratic

A second degree polynomial is called a quadratic.

Binomial

A polynomial with two terms is called a binomial.

Leading Term

The leading term contains the variable with the highest power.

It's good practice for this term to be first in the polynomial, and then order the rest of the terms in descending degree.

Multiplying

Each term is multiplied by each term in the second term, using the Distributive Property repeatedly. Then combine like terms.

\( \begin{array}{l l}f(x) &= (3x^2 - 2x + 1) \cdot (2x + 3) \\ & = 3x^2 \cdot (2x + 3) - 2x \cdot (2x + 3) + 1 \cdot (2x + 3) \\ & = 6x^3 + 9x^2 - 4x^2 - 6x + 2x + 3 \\ & = 6x^3 + 5x^2 - 4x + 3 \end{array} \)
