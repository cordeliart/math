Forest Algebra

a forest algebra \( (H,V,act,in_l,in_r) \) consists of:

  • horizontal monoid \((H,+)\) with identity “\(0\)”

  • vertical monoid \( (V,\cdot ) \) with identity “\(1\)”

  • a left-monoidal action of \(V\) on \(H\), \(act: H\times V \rightarrow H\)    (\( v \cdot h \in H\))

  • two operations \(in_l\) & \(in_r : H\rightarrow V\)    (\( v+h,h+v \in V\))

    ※ monoid morphisms! \(in(h_1+h_2)=in(h_1)in(h_2)\) and \(in(0)=1\)

A Note on (Polish) Notation
$$ qf_1f_2f_3 = f_3(f_2(f_1(q)))$$

Action on the right is easier to follow for linguistic purposes (reading left to right, applying automata from left to right, etc), so we write \(act(h,v)\) as \(vh\).

Forest Algebra
Forest Algebra Axioms

  • action: \( (v\cdot w) h = v(wh)\)

  • insertion: \( in_l(g)h=g+h \) and &bs \( in_r(g)h=h+g \)

  • faithfulness: for all distinct \(v,w\in V\), there exists an \(h\in H\) such that \(vh\neq wh\)

Morphisms

Morphism

a morphism from one forest algebra \((H,V)\) to another \( (G,W) \) is a pair of monoid morphisms \( (\alpha: H\rightarrow G, \beta: V\rightarrow W) \) such that \(\forall h\in H, \forall v\in V\):

  • \(\alpha (vh) = \beta(v) \alpha(h) \)

  • \( \beta\big(in_l(h) \big) = in_l\big( \alpha(h) \big) \) and \( \beta\big(in_r(h) \big) = in_r\big( \alpha(h) \big) \)

Given a morphism \(\beta\), \(\alpha\) can be determined using:

\( \alpha(h) = \alpha(h+0) = \alpha\big( in_l(h)0 \big) = \beta\big( in_l(h) \big) \alpha(0) \)

Free Forest Algebras

Free Forest Algebra

a free forest algebra \(A^\triangle\) over an alphabet \(A\)

  • horizontal monoid: set of forests over \(A\)

  • vertical monoid: set of contexts over \(A\)

  • action: substitution of forests in contexts

  • \(in_l\) and \(in_r\): transform a forest into a context by adding a hole to the right or left, respectively, of all its roots

Bojańczyk 3.6

The free forest algebra \(A^\triangle\) is a forest algebra.

For every forest algebra \((H, V )\), every function \(f : A \rightarrow V\) can be uniquely extended to a morphism \( (\alpha,\beta): A^\triangle\rightarrow (H,V) \) such that \(\beta(a(*)) = f(a)\) for every \(a \in A\).

Recognizing Morphism

A set \(L\) of \(A\)-forests is said to be recognized by a surjective morphism \( (\alpha, \beta) : A^\triangle \rightarrow (H, V)\) if \(L\) is the inverse image \(\alpha^{-1}(G)\) of some \(G \subseteq H\).

The morphism \( (\alpha, \beta) \) is said to recognize \(L\), the set \(G\) is called the accepting set, and \(L\) is said to be recognized by \((H, V)\).
