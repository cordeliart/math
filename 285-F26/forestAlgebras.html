Forest Algebras

Forest Algebra

a forest algebra \( (H,V,act,in_l,in_r) \) is:

  • horizontal monoid \((H,+)\) with identity \(0\)

  • vertical monoid \( (V,\cdot ) \) with identity \(1\)

  • one action \(act: H\times V \rightarrow H \text{ of } V \text{ on } H\), denoted \(vh\)

    instead of \(act(h,v)\), thanks french ppl :/

  • two operations \(in_l\) & \(in_r\) from \(H\rightarrow V\)

Forest Algebra
Forest Algebra Axioms

  • action: \( (v\cdot w) h = v(wh) \) -> often written \(vwh\)

  • insertion:

    \( in_l(g)h=g+h \)

    \( in_r(g)h=g+h \)

  • faithfulness: for all distinct \(v,w\in V\), \(\exists h\in H\) such that \(vh\neq wh\)
